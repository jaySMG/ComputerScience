#How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
Creating user stories helps organize user needs and wants in a cohesive manner.  It also helps sorts those needs by level of priority or difficulty, making it easier to work on the most important stuff first.

#How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I hope to incorporate adaptabilty into my future development work.  Too often i tend to be super rigid in my designs and ideas.  Im hoping that by taking what i have learned in this course, i can learn to break bigger projects down and be better able to adapt.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
It means to be open and honest with your team members.  Immediatly letting them know about any issues or problems that you ran into and being a team player for others issues.  Being accountable is also very important in being a good team member, accountability builds trust, and trust is a valued resource when it comes to team work.
